Places to visit in Mussoorie

  • Mall Road – the main walking street with shops, cafés and valley views.
  • Kempty Falls – a popular waterfall about 15 km from Mussoorie town.
  • Gun Hill – the second-highest point of Mussoorie, reached by ropeway or a short walk.
  • Lal Tibba – the highest viewpoint in Landour with views of the Himalayan peaks on clear days.
  • Company Garden – a well-kept garden, good for families with children.
  • Camel's Back Road – a quiet road for an evening walk and sunset views.
  • George Everest's House – a historic spot with wide views of the Doon valley.

Tips for a comfortable trip

  1. Start early on weekends and holidays – traffic near Mussoorie builds up after 10 am.
  2. Carry a light jacket even in summer; evenings in the hills are cool.
  3. If you feel motion sickness, sit in the front seat and keep the window open a little.
  4. Parking near Mall Road is limited – let the driver drop you and pick you up at a fixed point.
  5. In the rainy season (July–August), check the weather before you go.
