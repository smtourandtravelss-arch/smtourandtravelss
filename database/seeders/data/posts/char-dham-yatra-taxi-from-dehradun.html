The Char Dham Yatra – Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath – is one of the most important pilgrimages in India. Dehradun is a convenient starting point: it has an airport and a railway station, and the roads to all four dhams start close by. Travelling by private taxi lets your family move at its own pace, stop when needed and keep the same driver for the whole yatra. Here is a simple planning guide.

The usual Char Dham route

Pilgrims traditionally travel from west to east:

  1. Yamunotri – by road to Janki Chatti, then a trek of about 6 km.
  2. Gangotri – the temple can be reached by road.
  3. Kedarnath – by road to Sonprayag / Gaurikund, then a trek of about 16–18 km (pony, palki and helicopter options are also available).
  4. Badrinath – the temple can be reached by road.

A complete Char Dham Yatra by taxi usually takes 10 to 12 days from Dehradun. You can also book a Do Dham (Kedarnath and Badrinath) or a single dham trip.

When to go

The temples open around April–May and close around October–November every year; exact dates are announced by the temple committees. May–June and September–October are the most popular months. During the monsoon (July–August) landslides can close roads, so keep extra days in your plan.

Checklist before you leave

  • Complete the official Char Dham yatra registration of the Uttarakhand government for every passenger.
  • Carry original ID cards (Aadhaar or similar) for all travellers.
  • Pack warm clothes, a rain jacket, comfortable shoes and basic medicines.
  • Book hotels in advance during the peak season.
  • Elderly travellers should consult a doctor before trekking at high altitude.
